## Interpretation and limitations

The full model committed in every biased-input run, changed to the new focus in all ten switch runs, and released its commitment in all ten silence runs. One balanced run formed a temporary commitment, later released; none remained committed at the end. Balanced sampling is uniform in expectation, not exactly equal-frequency in finite samples.

The evidence-only control also changed focus in every switch run, with mean response 21.1 turns versus 28.0 for the full model. Thus this experiment does not demonstrate a feedback performance advantage: it exhibits additional inertia. Full-model final winners in dominant and switch regimes matched the recent-frequency baseline in all runs. A frequency-based explanation has not been excluded.

The context-gate comparison uses pair stimuli that readily provide diverse contexts; it is not a strong discriminator of that gate. Targeted context failures are covered by unit tests, not established as a general empirical benefit. The no-memory variant removes memory pressure from scores but retains evidence history for its gate.

These are descriptive results for specified seeds and defaults. There is no parameter sweep, significance claim, independent replication, consciousness result or proof of endogenous semantic identity. CI status is separate from these local results.
